MLIVE DIGITAL SOLUTIONS LLC / PRODUCT STRATEGY

From opportunity intelligence to transferable business infrastructure.

Watchtower connects source-backed deal research, qualified execution partners, and cash-first underwriting. We are building it first as a private owner decision desk—with the option to license the workflow, integrate it with a larger platform, or sell the underlying assets when the evidence supports it.

Possible ways to commercialize

Asset acquisition

Transfer assignable software rights and documented assets through a negotiated agreement after IP and source-data diligence.

White-label license

Offer a secured private version to qualifying business development organizations or agencies, with contractual support and privacy obligations.

Integration license

License a tested opportunity-screening and partner-matching workflow to an established procurement or operations platform.

Operating partnership

Use Watchtower within a measured, compliant deal-orchestration partnership with clear owner authority and financial terms.

Transparency matters. No paying users, vendor network, verified profitable deals, proprietary protected datasets or acquisition valuation are claimed at this stage. The code is hosted in a public repository, and the public frontend is not a secure private customer platform. The next commercial milestone is real evidence of demand and execution quality, not a bigger demo.
