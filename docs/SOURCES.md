# Source catalog and research discipline

## Government and official

- **SAM.gov public opportunities API:** https://open.gsa.gov/api/get-opportunities-public-api/ — documented API; needs key and backend adapter. Opportunity detail needs current solicitation check.
- **SAM.gov:** https://sam.gov/opportunities — manual browse and source-of-truth for federal notices.
- **Michigan Vendor Opportunity Dashboard:** https://www.michigan.gov/dtmb/procurement/contractconnect/vendor-opportunity-dashboard — planning and discovery; verify live bids in SIGMA Vendor Self Service.
- **City of Detroit OCP:** https://detroitmi.gov/departments/office-chief-financial-officer/ocfo-divisions/office-contracting-and-procurement — official municipal procurement and portal links.
- **USAspending:** https://api.usaspending.gov/ — historical award and spend research, **not proof of an open bid**.
- **SBA Small Business Search:** https://search.certifications.sba.gov/ — candidate research / certifications subject to current verification.
- **GSA opportunities guide:** https://www.gsa.gov/small-business/find-opportunities — partnership and government-market resources.
- **13 CFR 125.6:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-125.6 — limitations on subcontracting; solicitor-specific counsel/review required.

## Private/digital service

- Authorized direct RFPs, referral clients, official customer website notices.
- Clutch: https://clutch.co/ — *human-assisted* vendor discovery. No unauthorized scraping, republishing or fabricated firm ratings.
- Supplier official websites, published portfolio, references, written quotes, availability and contract terms.

## Evidence standards

Every real candidate should carry source URL, observed date, source type, buyer/issuer, deadline and origin. Mark unknown amounts as unknown (do **not** substitute LLM assumptions). Distinguish primary source, secondary report, and hypothesis. An article about a trend does not equal customer demand.

Public directories are research leads only. Never claim partner has agreed to a project without contacting them and receiving a quote.
