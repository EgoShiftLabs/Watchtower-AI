## Chain-of-title to collect before offering an asset transfer
- Document whether MLive Digital Solutions LLC or the founder personally owns each copyrightable component; execute assignment documentation if needed.
- Record all outside contributors, generated assets, commissioned work and source licenses.
- Identify and document use of AI coding services and terms relevant to future diligence.
- Search for conflicting marks before investing in trademark protection.
- Confirm domain/control and account transfer options and restrictions.
- Maintain immutable release tags and a reproducible build.
- Have qualified attorneys review licenses, sale/assignment terms and client data transfer.
- Never list unverified exclusivity, proprietary feeds, customer lists or vendor relationships as owned assets.

## Public/private separation plan
Keep public: design showcase, synthetic records, general workflow, public landing page.
Move private before collecting: actual deal dossiers, vendor quotations, customer correspondence, secrets, strategic scoring experiments, negotiated rates, contracts and operations history.
A future private repo will not erase copies of already-public source code.
