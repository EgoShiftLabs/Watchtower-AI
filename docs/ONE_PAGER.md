# WATCHTOWER AI
## Private opportunity intelligence. Execution partner discovery. Cash-controlled business orchestration.

**Operator / concept owner:** MLive Digital Solutions LLC (confirm written IP chain-of-title prior to any transfer).
**Public working prototype:** https://egoshiftlabs.github.io/Watchtower-AI/
**Repository:** https://github.com/EgoShiftLabs/Watchtower-AI

### The problem
Small operators can identify business opportunities but lack time to research buyers, screen suppliers, check contract risks and model upfront funding. Lead databases sell lists; sourcing a capable subcontractor and underwriting the actual deal is a different problem.

### The product
A structured human-in-the-loop intelligence desk connecting:
1. **Demand discovery** from official procurement sources and authorized direct requests.
2. **Evidence records** about source, buyer, scope and closing dates.
3. **Execution partner shortlist** backed by skills, availability, quote evidence and checks.
4. **Cash and contribution underwriting**, with no assumed-free client deposits.
5. **Executive decision queue**, capped at five priorities, with explicit no-go conditions.

### What exists today
A public GitHub Pages UI, editable manual research records, separate synthetic demo data, deterministic finance and matching rules, GitHub Actions checks, an optional SAM.gov public-feed adapter requiring a separately configured API secret, cash-flow scenario, source directory and product workflows. Local data is not encrypted or synced.

### What does not exist
Secure backend, automated vendor qualification, commercial revenues, paying customer base, validated sourcing performance, transferable vendor contracts or enterprise support. The $2,500 monthly contribution goal belongs to the founder's future operations and is **not an observed result**.

### Initial focus
Digital services 60% / government opportunities 25% / AI venture hypotheses 15% (research priorities). Validate 20 actual leads and two written vendor quotes before heavy automation spend.

### Why a buyer might care
An integration-ready workflow that evaluates execution feasibility and affordability **after** opportunity discovery, instead of producing another unqualified list of leads.

### Potential transaction models
Asset sale, white-label license, software component license or carefully governed operating partnership. Commercial terms and valuation require independent diligence.

### Next proof
A legitimate quote-backed opportunity and a completed transaction with independently reconciled contribution and documented founder hours.

**Contact and transaction terms:** To be supplied through an authorized business contact. No price or buyer interest is claimed.
